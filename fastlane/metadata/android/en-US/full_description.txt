Take a photo of your stuff, frame each item with your finger, put it in the box, type in the number. With every frame your head gets a little clearer.

And you get to rethink: your boxes don't need themes anymore. Instead of a tool box, a toy box and "miscellaneous" there are just numbers — you pack what fits into the box, not necessarily what belongs together thematically. That way you use every box down to the last corner. boxorg remembers where things are.

Looking for something? Type a term or a box number — boxorg shows you the photo and the box it is in.
